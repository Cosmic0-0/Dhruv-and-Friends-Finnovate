Protect yourself from fraud

MCB will never ask you to share your OTP, PIN, password or card CVV by SMS, email or phone. If someone asks you for your OTP, it is a scam. Do not click links in unexpected messages. Always log in by typing internet.mcb.mu yourself.

If you think your account has been compromised, call our 24/7 fraud line immediately on 202 6060 so we can block your card.

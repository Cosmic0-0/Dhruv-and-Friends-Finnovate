# Finnovate Web and AI Hackathon 2026 — Final Project Evaluation Guidelines

## Evaluation Process
Each finalist team presents a live demonstration followed by a Q&A session with the
jury. Evaluation focuses on quality of final implementation, effectiveness against the
assigned challenge statement, and the team's ability to communicate and justify its
solution.

## Important Note on Originality
Multiple finalist teams may choose the same challenge statement. Originality matters:
jury assesses how each team differentiates its solution from others addressing the
same challenge, including uniqueness of features, implementation, user experience,
technical approach, and overall value proposition. Teams don't need an entirely
different challenge statement, but must show a distinctive, innovative solution.

## Evaluation Criteria — 100 Marks

### 1. Solution Implementation & Functionality — 35 marks
- Whether the prototype works successfully during the live demonstration
- Whether core features and objectives are implemented
- Quality of technical implementation and overall reliability
- Appropriate use of technologies and development tools
- Whether the solution demonstrates a polished and functional product

### 2. Innovation & Technical Excellence — 25 marks
- Originality of the solution
- Creativity in addressing the assigned challenge
- Innovative use of AI, web technologies, and FinTech concepts
- Technical depth and complexity, where appropriate
- How effectively the project distinguishes itself from other teams on the same
  challenge statement

### 3. Impact & Problem Solving — 25 marks
- How well the solution addresses the assigned challenge statement
- Value created for users and stakeholders
- Practical applicability in real-world settings
- Potential for scalability and long-term sustainability
- Potential economic, social, or financial impact

### 4. Presentation & Demonstration — 15 marks
- Clarity and organisation of the presentation
- Quality and effectiveness of the live demonstration
- Communication skills and confidence
- Ability to explain technical decisions and design choices
- Effectiveness in responding to jury questions
- Team coordination during the presentation
